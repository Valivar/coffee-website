FROM BEAN TO CUP

    Stage 01 // Origin

    Red Ripe Coffee Cherry

    Hand-picked at peak ripeness from our high-altitude estate.

  2. 02
    Stage 02 // Depulping

    Wet Processing & Depulping

    Cherries are depulped to separate the fruit from the parchment-covered beans.

  3. 03
    Stage 03 // Sun-Drying

    Sun-Dried Parchment

    Dried on raised beds and turned by hand for even, gentle drying.

  4. 04
    Stage 04 // Curing

    Curing & Hulling

    Parchment is hulled away and beans are graded by size and quality.

  5. 05
    Stage 05 // Roasting

    Small-Batch Roasting

    Roasted in small batches to match each bean's profile.

  6. 06
    Stage 06 // Crack & Grind

    Cracked & Ground

    Ground evenly and packed straight away to lock in aroma.

  7. 07
    Stage 07 // Packaging

    Sealed Fresh

    Sealed at the source so it reaches you tasting like it just left the roaster.

Frequently Asked Questions

01Estate OriginWhere does our coffee come from?

From our own estate, where we grow both Arabica and Robusta.

02Cultivation & HeritageDo you grow your own coffee?

Yes. Growing it ourselves gives us control from cultivation to processing.

03In-House ProcessingWhere is the coffee processed?

At our own facility, with an in-house pulping and curing unit.

04Quality & CraftsmanshipHow do you maintain quality?

We manage growing, harvesting, pulping, curing, roasting and grinding ourselves.

05Arabica vs RobustaWhat is the difference?

Arabica is smoother and more aromatic; Robusta is stronger and fuller. We offer both.
